How To Evaluate Calibration Model

The Concepts 3D Exposure Calibration prints six test tiles using increasing exposure times. Tile 1 uses the shortest exposure and Tile 6 uses the longest.

After printing, wash and dry all six tiles thoroughly, then compare the fine details to determine which exposure gives the best overall result.

  • Concepts3D logo: Look for crisp, complete lettering without bloating or merging.
  • Holes and pins: A balanced number of visible holes and pins indicates a good exposure.
  • Three triangles: The tips should meet cleanly in the center without a gap or blob.
  • 6 mm square: The square should measure close to 6.0 mm.
  • Fine lines and gaps: Raised lines should remain separate while fine gaps remain open.

Once you identify the best tile, use that tile's exposure time as the normal-layer exposure for your resin profile. If Tile 1 or Tile 6 is clearly best, run the calibration again with the starting exposure shifted so the best result falls closer to the middle of the range.

Check out J3D's instructions for a guide on how to read the Boxes of Calibration.

Once you find the optimal exposure time, you can print another type of calibration model to verify it, or create a new resin profile with the normal exposure time you determined here.
